About Yingmotors

Shandong Yingmotors Co.,Ltd supplies commercial vehicles and construction machinery to overseas buyers. We work from Liangshan County, Jining City, Shandong Province — a concentrated market for heavy trucks, trailers and used excavators.

Current stock includes new trailers and tricycles, used HOWO, SITRAK and SHACMAN trucks, coaches, mixers, SDLG loaders, a HOWO jetting-vacuum truck, and Komatsu excavators. Buyers typically come from Africa, the Middle East, Central Asia and Southeast Asia.
