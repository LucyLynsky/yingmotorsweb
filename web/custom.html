Customize products

Some chassis and new vehicles can be configured for your market — tank size, body type, colour, logo and special equipment.

Used stock is sold as photographed. For new trucks, trailers, tricycles and selected special vehicles, we can discuss a custom build instead of a standard listing.

Tell Kate the chassis you prefer, the job the vehicle must do, destination country, and any must-have options. We reply with a configuration, EXW / FOB / CIF price and production time.

Typical custom items include dump body length, tanker volume, high-pressure jetting setup, colour and your company marking. Final specification is confirmed in writing before production or conversion.

1. Send the job

Model family, payload, tank or body size, and destination country.

2. Confirm the spec

We quote EXW / FOB / CIF and share photos or drawings before you pay.

3. Produce and load

You approve the finished unit photos, then we handle export documents with your forwarder.
